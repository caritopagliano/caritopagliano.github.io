Case study 01 of 05

Self-Service Triage Tool

A bilingual guide applicants complete before booking, so they take the right path and bring the right documents.

Key result
Applicants arrive prepared
Languages
English and Spanish

01Problem

Applicants booked appointments without knowing what they needed, or emailed staff again and again with basic questions. Appointments were wasted and the front desk was overloaded.

02What I built

A bilingual (English / Spanish) self-service guide applicants complete before booking. It asks which document they have now and sends them down the right path: book online, book online and bring extra proof of identity, or email first for a records check.
