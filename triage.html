01Problem

Applicants booked appointments without knowing what they needed, or emailed staff again and again with basic questions. Appointments were wasted and the front desk was overloaded.

02What I built

Applicants answer one or two questions about the document they have now. The guide then sends them down one of three paths: book online, book online and bring extra proof of identity, or email first so staff can check their details. Everything is available in English and Spanish.

Key decision

Asks which document applicants hold, not which procedure they need → most people don't know the procedure name.

03Result

Checked before they book

Before, applicants booked without knowing what they needed. Now each one is sent down the right path first, and anyone with an older document has their details checked before they take an appointment.
