Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

A one-page SOP, written with AI assistance, that ties all the tools together and gives full visibility of every document.

  • Self-initiated
  • Designed and built by me
  • AI-assisted build
  • Whole workflow built in ~1 week
  • In daily use
Key result
4 stages, end to end
Used for
Onboarding and handovers

01Problem

Process knowledge lived in people's heads, so onboarding and handovers were slow, and there was no single view of which documents never arrived, which were received and which hadn't been collected.

02What I built

A one-page SOP, written with AI assistance, that ties all the tools together. It gives full visibility of:

  • Documents that never arrived
  • Documents received
  • Documents not yet collected
Key decision

One page that follows the document's journey and shows which tool to use at each step → anyone new can run the process from day one.

03How it works

  1. 1

    Daily Intake

    Load the day's cash report into the Formatter, confirm any flagged cases, and paste the result into Case Tracking.

  2. 2

    Desk Review

    Add the final ID number when a provisional one is replaced, and add the applicant's email for arrival notices. Formula columns are never edited by hand.

  3. 3

    Courier Arrival

    Record the pouch date and reference, then scan each document. Documents found in the tracker are marked "At the desk". Unknown documents are flagged for separate handling.

  4. 4

    Document Collection

    The applicant signs a paper slip at the counter. Staff keep the barcode stub and scan all stubs in one batch at closing, and the case is archived automatically.

  • Design ruleNo computer at the counter: slips are scanned in one batch at closing, so service stays fast.
  • Design ruleFormula columns are locked.
  • Design ruleUnknown documents are flagged, not mixed in.

Now used for onboarding new staff and for handovers.
