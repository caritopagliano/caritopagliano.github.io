The real tool covers 17 procedures, including children's documents and urgent cases. The demo shows 4. Requirements are illustrative.

01Problem

Confirmation emails were long blocks of unformatted text. Applicants didn't read them, so they arrived without the required documents and didn't reply with the information we asked for. Each email was also copied from a Word template, pasted into the webmail, then reformatted and personalised by hand.

02What I built

Staff choose the language (English or Spanish), the applicant's name, the date and the time, then click the procedure. The email, subject line included, is ready to paste into the webmail. Requirements appear as a short checklist, and the information we need back sits in its own box.

Key decision

Requirements as a short checklist, generated in one click → no copy-pasting or reformatting for staff, and nothing for applicants to miss.

03Result

~3–5 min → under 30 sec per email

That saving repeats across 25–50 emails a week, plus urgent appointments. The checklist and the reply box make it clear what to bring and what to send back.
